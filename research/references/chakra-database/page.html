Boruto Database: Rasengan

Rasengan

Classification: Ninjutsu
Wielder(s): Minato Namikaze, Jiraiya, Naruto Uzumaki, Konohamaru Sarutobi, Boruto Uzumaki, others
A technique that attacks with a ball of chakra compressed and rotated in the palm of one's hand.
It was originally developed by Minato Namikaze, who is said to have taken inspiration from the Biju Bombs used by Tailed Beasts.
